# Changelog

## v1.0.0: The map comes first

Atlas is rebuilt around one card that sits over a map that never leaves.

### New
- **Light and dark themes, with a switch in the footer.** Atlas follows your system until you flip it
- **One search finds both spots and places.** Pick a place and the map flies there
- **Pins carry each spot's score in its category colour.** Select one to read its name
- **The planner suggests what to ask next** after each answer
- **On a phone, the list is a sheet you drag up over the map**
- **The footer names who made Atlas and which version you're on**, linked to this page

### Changed
- **A spot's details open inside the card**, with the week's hours, scores, tags and reviews together
- **Add a spot opens a form over whatever you're viewing**, and fills the screen on a phone
- **Opening a row in the list view reveals new details** instead of repeating the row
- **The map moves only as far as it needs to** to keep a selected spot clear of the cards
- **The chat opens as a card that grows from its own button**
- **Zoom and your location are buttons in Atlas's own style**, not the map library's

## v0.3.0: Atmo's design system

Atlas takes on the colour, type and spacing of Atmo's design system. Nothing about how it works changes.

### Changed
- **Atlas is set in Satoshi**, with colours and spacing drawn from Atmo's design system
- **Icons replace emoji, and each category gets its own map pin**
- **Text follows six consistent sizes** on every screen

### Fixed
- **The list view fits a phone's width** without scrolling sideways
- **Keyboard focus always shows, and reduced motion is respected throughout**
- **The map no longer draws over the app's cards and menus**

## v0.2.0: Atlas leaves Replit

Same app, new home.

### Changed
- **Atlas runs on Cloudflare with a shared Supabase database**, and no longer depends on Replit

### Fixed
- **AI summaries save again** after the move to the new database

## v0.1.0: First release

A community map of places to work remotely, starting in Austin.

### New
- **A map of work spots in Austin**, seeded with local cafés, libraries, coworking spaces and parks
- **A list view you can filter by city, category and tag**
- **Scores for food, wifi, hours, accessibility, outlets and atmosphere**, rated by visitors
- **AI summaries of what people say about each spot**
- **A planner that builds your work day** from the spots on the map
- **Crowd reports show how busy a spot is right now**
- **Opening hours, and addresses that complete as you type** when you add a spot
- **Directions to any spot in Google Maps or Apple Maps**
